Add your profile photo here, named exactly:

    profile.jpg   or   profile.jpeg   or   profile.png   or   profile.webp

Any of those four extensions works -- the site automatically tries each
one in turn and uses whichever file it finds. You only need to get the
name "profile" right, not the extension.

Recommended: a square, well-lit headshot, at least 500x500px.

Until this file exists, the site automatically shows your "AJ"
monogram instead -- nothing breaks either way.
